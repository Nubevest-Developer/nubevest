Scope & regulatory boundaries

What Nubevest and its associated businesses do — and where formal regulated authority sits with third parties.
  • NDIS. Nubevest supports NDIS providers with websites, digital growth, CRM/operations software and workforce services. Nubevest is not itself a registered NDIS provider unless separately disclosed. Participant-facing NDIS services are delivered by registered providers.
  • Education / RTO. Skill to Upskill helps individuals understand pathways, prepare evidence and engage with an appropriate Registered Training Organisation. Skill to Upskill is not itself an RTO, trainer, assessor or qualification issuer. Formal assessment and qualifications are controlled by the relevant RTO.
  • Recruitment & workforce (Workers Australia). Where a labour-hire, migration or specialist workforce licence applies, the licence number, licensee, jurisdiction and scope will be published here and in the proposal after current primary-source verification.
  • Dubai property (Kangar Dubai Experts). Regulated real-estate activities are performed by the UAE-licensed contracting entity disclosed on invoices and agreements. A business name is not proof of a real-estate licence.
  • Long-term vehicle rentals (NubeR). Availability, eligibility, insurance, bond and rental terms are confirmed by the lessor before an offer is published. The lessor of record appears in the rental agreement.
  • Manufacturer relationships (Monitormart). Product references (for example Yealink and Logitech) are used only as product names. Manufacturer partner marks, authorised reseller badges and distributor status appear only after written verification.
Accuracy commitment. Nubevest does not publish licence numbers, partner badges, guaranteed outcomes, review counts or performance metrics without current primary-source evidence. Where a detail is still being verified, this page and the associated proposal will say so explicitly.
