Plannor

Platform &mdash; a platform by Nubevest

NDIS provider management software designed to bring participants, staff, rostering, timesheets, service agreements, documents, incidents, complaints and billing into a connected environment.

Request a Plannor Demonstration Back to Nubevest ecosystem
P
Role inside Nubevest

Plannor is a Nubevest-built platform for Australian NDIS providers seeking a clearer operational system. It sits inside Nubevest’s NDIS provider solution alongside websites, CRM and marketing where relevant.

What customers can buy
  • Participant, staff, rostering and shift management modules (as released).
  • Service agreements, documents and compliance tracking (as released).
  • Timesheets, incidents, complaints and operational reporting (as released).
  • Billing and claims workflows aligned to NDIS operational needs (as released).
  • Implementation, onboarding and ongoing support through Nubevest.
Best-fit customers

Australian NDIS providers currently juggling spreadsheets, disconnected tools and manual admin, and providers scaling up who need one operational system to work from.

Website & enquiry route.
Placed under Platforms. Cross-linked from the NDIS Providers industry page.
Accuracy boundary
Plannor launches only verified, currently available modules. Functions still in development or planned are labelled as such and never described as available. Provider-specific fit, migration and data-scope are confirmed in the proposal.
One accountable Nubevest relationship.
You engage Nubevest — not Plannor as a separate company. Every proposal identifies who’s responsible for what.
