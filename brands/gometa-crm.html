GoMeta CRM

Platform &mdash; a platform by Nubevest

A practical CRM and workflow platform for capturing enquiries, assigning ownership, tracking stages, automating follow-up and giving teams a clearer view of customers and work.

Review My Enquiry &amp; Follow-up Process Back to Nubevest ecosystem
G
Role inside Nubevest

GoMeta CRM is used by Nubevest to solve the “disconnected inbox and spreadsheet” problem for service businesses. It sits inside the CRM & Automation solution and is introduced when a manageable, structured CRM is a better fit than a heavier enterprise product.

What customers can buy
  • Enquiry capture from website forms, phone and email.
  • Contact, deal and pipeline management with stage tracking.
  • Task assignment, reminders and follow-up automation.
  • Email and SMS workflows tied to real customer stages.
  • Reporting on enquiries, response times and pipeline health.
  • Implementation, workflow design and ongoing support through Nubevest.
Best-fit customers

Service businesses that need a manageable alternative to disconnected inboxes and spreadsheets — NDIS providers, builders and trades, professional services, inspectors and small growing offices.

Website & enquiry route.

Accuracy boundary
GoMeta CRM is a practical CRM tool, not a replacement for regulated case-management or clinical systems. Data scope, integrations, retention and export terms are confirmed in the proposal.
One accountable Nubevest relationship.
