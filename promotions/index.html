08Artwork and support

Print-ready, with a person checking.

Every file is reviewed by our team before it goes to print; automated checks never have the final say. If we adjust your artwork or design it for you, we’ll send a proof for your approval first.

Before you send artwork

  1. 01File type. PDF, PNG or JPG. A print-ready PDF usually gives the best result.
  2. 02Front and back. For double-sided products, supply the front and back as separate files.
  3. 03Size, bleed and safe area. Match the product’s dimensions, extend backgrounds past the trim edge, and keep text and logos inside the safe area.
  4. 04Resolution and colour. Use high-resolution images at the final print size. We’ll share colour guidance for your product.
