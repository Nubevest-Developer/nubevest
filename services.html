What We Offer

Complete Carpentry & Building Solutions

We deliver end-to-end carpentry and building material solutions with precision, quality, and reliability that Australian builders trust.

01

First Fix Carpentry

We specialise in structural carpentry, including wall framing, roof framing, floor systems, and timber structures. Our skilled team ensures every project is built with accuracy and in accordance with Australian building standards.

  • Wall & Roof Framing
  • Floor Systems
  • Timber Structures
02

Second Fix Carpentry

Our second fix carpentry services include the installation of doors, skirting boards, architraves, wardrobes, stair components, shelving, and other finishing elements that complete your project to the highest standard.

  • Doors & Architraves
  • Wardrobes & Shelving
  • Skirting & Stairs
03

Truss Supply

We supply premium engineered roof trusses and wall frames manufactured to Australian standards, providing strength, durability, and efficient installation for projects of all sizes.

  • Engineered Roof Trusses
  • Wall Frames
  • Australian Standards
04

Building Materials Supply

Competent supplies a wide range of quality building materials to builders, developers, contractors, and owner-builders. We are committed to dependable supply, competitive pricing, and timely delivery.

  • Quality Materials
  • Competitive Pricing
  • Timely Delivery
05

Eaves Supply & Installation

We offer complete supply and installation of eaves and external finishing components, ensuring a durable and professional finish that enhances the appearance and performance of every build.

  • Complete Supply
  • Professional Install
  • Durable Finish
06

Estimating Services

Our experienced estimating team provides accurate material take-offs, cost estimates, and quantity schedules, helping clients plan projects efficiently and prepare competitive tenders.

  • Material Take-offs
  • Cost Estimates
  • Quantity Schedules
