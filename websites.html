What’s included in every plan

Every website package includes the essentials to get your business online with a professional, mobile-friendly website.

Mobile-Friendly Design

Built using an approved template, optimised for phones, tablets and desktops. Fast-loading and accessible.

Hosting & SSL Certificate

Secure, reliable hosting and SSL included for the first 12 months. Your visitors see the padlock from day one.

Basic Search Metadata

Page titles, descriptions and structure set up for Google. A solid starting point for search visibility.

Contact Form & Revisions

A working contact form and two rounds of design revisions included. We build it right, together.

Not included in the base price

  • Domain registration
  • Premium plugins or licences
  • eCommerce platform subscriptions
  • Transaction or payment gateway fees
  • Full copywriting or new branding
  • Bespoke features or custom development
